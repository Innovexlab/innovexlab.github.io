About

A molecular testing lab in Gainesville you can call directly

InnovexLab is a molecular testing laboratory on NW 23rd Avenue in Gainesville, Florida.

What we do

Innovex tests for respiratory, GI, urinary, wound, STI, women’s health and nail fungal infections, plus antibiotic-resistance genes.

Our panels use molecular methods: they read organisms and resistance genes directly from the specimen, without waiting for culture growth. That gives the ordering clinician information to weigh alongside culture and the clinical exam.

How we work with practices

We don’t ask you to switch labs. A practice or facility starts with the one panel that fits a question it sees often, keeps its current lab for everything else, and talks to the lab directly when something comes up.

What we’ll tell you plainly

  • A positive PCR doesn’t by itself diagnose a symptomatic infection.
  • A resistance gene doesn’t by itself determine susceptibility or treatment.
  • Molecular testing works alongside culture and clinical judgment, not instead of them.
