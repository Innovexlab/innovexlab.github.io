The InnovexLab laboratory

InnovexLab performs PCR testing for respiratory, gastrointestinal, urinary, wound, STI, women’s health and nail infections, with antibiotic-resistance gene detection.

Molecular methods identify organisms and resistance genes directly from the specimen, without waiting for culture growth. Results are reported to the ordering provider for interpretation alongside culture and the clinical exam.

Laboratory-developed testing

InnovexLab’s UTI test was developed, and its performance characteristics were determined, by InnovexLab. It has not been cleared or approved by the FDA. InnovexLab is regulated under CLIA as qualified to perform high-complexity testing.

Working with practices and facilities

Clients typically begin with a single panel for a frequent testing question and keep their current laboratory for other testing. Ordering clinicians and administrators have direct access to InnovexLab.
