What a molecular result adds

Culture tells you what grows. Molecular testing reads the genetic material that is already in the sample.

Read directly from the specimen

PCR detects organisms and resistance genes in the sample itself. It doesn’t have to wait for anything to grow first.

Information for a decision

The ordering clinician can use it while weighing precautions, cohorting or an antibiotic choice, alongside the rest of the clinical picture.

Honest about its limits

A positive PCR doesn’t by itself diagnose a symptomatic infection, and a resistance gene doesn’t by itself decide treatment. Culture and clinical review still matter.
