Molecular infection testing for long-term care facilities and medical practices

PCR panels for urinary, wound, respiratory, gastrointestinal, STI, women’s health and nail infections, with antibiotic-resistance gene detection. Organisms and resistance genes are identified directly from the specimen, without waiting for culture growth.

Testing services

Target menus, accepted specimen types and collection requirements are provided by the laboratory during consultation.

  • UTISymptomatic patients with a suspected urinary source.
  • WoundNon-healing or clinically infected wounds.
  • RespiratorySymptomatic respiratory illness and facility clusters.
  • Gastrointestinal (GI)Acute diarrheal illness and suspected unit outbreaks.
  • Nail fungalSuspected onychomycosis before antifungal therapy.
  • Women’s healthVaginitis and vaginosis with overlapping causes.
  • STISymptomatic patients and recommended screening.
  • Antibiotic resistanceResistance genes reported with organism results.

Molecular results, used alongside culture

Direct detection

Targets are identified from the specimen itself, independent of culture growth.

Resistance genes

Resistance markers are reported with organism results to inform antimicrobial selection.

Clinical context

Results support, and do not replace, culture, susceptibility testing and clinical assessment. Limitations

Getting started

Facilities and practices can add InnovexLab alongside their current reference laboratory, beginning with a single panel.

  1. Consultation

    Select the panel that fits your most frequent testing question.

  2. Specimen review

    The ordering clinician reviews the panel, specimen and collection requirements with the laboratory.

  3. Logistics, reporting and billing

    Your administrator confirms specimen transport, result reporting to the ordering provider, and the billing path.

  4. Go-live

    Complete the setup paperwork and choose the date of your first order.

Getting started in detail
