InnovexLab Wound panel

Bacteria, including anaerobes, fungi and viruses identified directly from wound swabs or tissue, with a reflex antibiotic-resistance gene panel.

General medical information for education only, not medical advice. Targets as listed on InnovexLab’s requisition forms (wound requisition form). Detection of a target does not by itself establish infection or determine treatment; results are interpreted by the ordering clinician with the clinical picture and, where indicated, culture and susceptibility testing.

When to consider testing

Wounds with clinical signs of infection: spreading erythema, warmth, swelling, purulence, increasing pain, malodor or a stalled wound. Collect after cleansing and debridement, from viable tissue where possible.

Interpretation

Chronic wounds are colonized by many organisms; detection is interpreted with the clinical exam. A resistance gene cannot be assigned to one organism in a mixed specimen.
