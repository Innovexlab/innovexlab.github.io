InnovexLab Respiratory panel

Respiratory viruses, bacteria and Pneumocystis identified directly from respiratory swabs, with a Basic Flu option and a reflex resistance panel.

General medical information for education only, not medical advice. Targets as listed on InnovexLab’s requisition forms (respiratory requisition form). Detection of a target does not by itself establish infection or determine treatment; results are interpreted by the ordering clinician with the clinical picture and, where indicated, culture and susceptibility testing.

When to consider testing

Symptomatic respiratory illness (cough, fever, sore throat, shortness of breath), particularly clusters of cases in a facility where results guide isolation, cohorting and antiviral or antibiotic decisions.

Interpretation

A viral result can support withholding antibiotics. Bacteria detected in upper-airway specimens may reflect colonization.
