InnovexLab Nail fungal panel

Dermatophytes, non-dermatophyte molds and yeasts that cause onychomycosis, identified directly from nail specimens.

General medical information for education only, not medical advice. Targets as listed on InnovexLab’s requisition forms (nail requisition form). Detection of a target does not by itself establish infection or determine treatment; results are interpreted by the ordering clinician with the clinical picture and, where indicated, culture and susceptibility testing.

When to consider testing

Nail thickening, discoloration, brittleness or onycholysis where a fungal cause is suspected; confirmation is recommended before systemic antifungal therapy.

Interpretation

Non-dermatophyte molds are also frequent contaminants; repeat detection or clinical correlation supports a causal role.
