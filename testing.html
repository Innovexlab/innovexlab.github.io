Testing

Molecular panels for the infection questions your team sees

Innovex tests for respiratory, GI, urinary, wound, STI, women’s health and nail fungal infections, plus antibiotic-resistance genes. Each panel reads its targets directly from the specimen, without waiting for growth.

What a molecular result can’t tell you on its own

  • PCR detects genetic material. A positive result doesn’t by itself establish a symptomatic infection; colonization is possible.
  • A detected resistance gene doesn’t by itself determine susceptibility or treatment. A gene that isn’t detected doesn’t prove the organism is susceptible.
  • A negative result rules out only the targets on that panel, not every possible cause.
  • When a specimen contains more than one organism, a detected resistance gene can’t be assigned to a specific organism.
  • Culture and clinical assessment still matter. Outside recommended screening, such as STI screening, testing is for patients with symptoms, ordered on clinical judgment.
