Testing services

PCR infection testing

Molecular panels for urinary, wound, respiratory, gastrointestinal, STI, women’s health and nail infections, with antibiotic-resistance gene detection. Targets are identified directly from the specimen, without waiting for culture growth.

Testing areas

Target menus, accepted specimen types and collection requirements are provided by the laboratory during consultation.

UTI

Indication
Urinary symptoms, or fever or other systemic signs without another likely source, where the clinician suspects a urinary source.
Clinical use
Organism and resistance-gene results to support antimicrobial decisions while culture and clinical assessment proceed.

Interpretation and limitations

  • PCR detects genetic material. A positive result does not by itself establish symptomatic infection; colonization is possible.
  • A detected resistance gene does not by itself determine susceptibility or treatment, and an undetected gene does not prove susceptibility.
  • A negative result excludes only the targets on that panel.
  • When a specimen contains more than one organism, a resistance gene cannot be assigned to a specific organism.
  • Results are interpreted with culture and clinical assessment. Outside recommended screening, such as STI screening, testing is for symptomatic patients, ordered on clinical judgment.
