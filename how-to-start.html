Four steps to your first order

  1. Choose one panel

    Start with the testing question your team sees most often, such as a urinary, wound or respiratory question. One panel is enough to begin.

    See testing areas

  2. Your clinician confirms the specimen

    The ordering clinician reviews the panel with the lab: what it detects, the specimen it needs and how it’s collected.

  3. Confirm logistics, results and billing

    Your administrator or practice manager reviews three things with us: how specimens get from your building to our lab, how results reach the ordering provider, and the billing path for your patients.

  4. Sign setup and set a start date

    Complete the setup paperwork, receive your startup collection supplies, and choose the day you’ll send your first appropriate order.

No cost to get started.

Onboarding is free, and we provide the collection supplies needed to start using our lab. You can keep your current lab and use Innovex as an additional option.

Tests are billed separately.

Who should be in the review

  • The ordering clinician, to confirm the panel and specimen fit.
  • The operational lead (administrator, director of nursing or practice manager), to confirm logistics, results and billing.

Fifteen minutes is enough for one panel.

Questions practice managers ask first

Do we have to stop using our current lab?

No. Start by adding Innovex for one panel. You can keep your current lab and use Innovex as an additional option.

What does it cost to get started?

Onboarding is free, and we provide the collection supplies needed to start using our lab. Ordered tests are billed separately, and we go over the billing path with you before the first order.

How is testing billed?

For Medicare Part B and insured patients, we bill the payer directly, with no invoice to your facility. Residents in a covered Medicare Part A skilled nursing stay fall under consolidated billing: the facility, not Medicare, pays the lab for those tests. We review that arrangement with your administrator before you order.

Uninsured? Ask us about self-pay options.

How do specimens get to the lab?

Ask us how specimens get from your building to our lab.

Where do results go?

Results go to the ordering provider. We’ll confirm how your team receives them during setup.

What specimen does each panel need?

The lab director reviews accepted specimens and collection steps for your panel with your ordering clinician. We’d rather confirm it with you directly than have a specimen rejected.

Who do we call with a question?

The lab, directly: (352) 328-3066 or info@innovex-lab.com.
