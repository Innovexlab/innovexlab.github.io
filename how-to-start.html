Getting started

Getting started with InnovexLab

InnovexLab can be added alongside your current reference laboratory. Onboarding follows four steps, beginning with a single panel.

Request a consultation

Existing clients: Client Portal

Onboarding

  1. Consultation

    Select the panel that fits your most frequent testing question, such as urinary, wound or respiratory infection. One panel is sufficient to begin.

    Testing services

  2. Specimen review

    The ordering clinician reviews the panel with the laboratory: its targets, the specimen it requires and the collection procedure.

  3. Logistics, reporting and billing

    Your administrator or practice manager confirms specimen transport to the laboratory, result reporting to the ordering provider, and the billing path for your patients.

  4. Go-live

    Complete the setup paperwork, receive startup collection supplies, and choose the date of your first order.

No-cost onboarding

Onboarding is free, and we provide the collection supplies needed to begin. Your current laboratory relationship can stay in place.

Tests are billed separately.

Consultation attendees

  • Ordering clinician: confirms panel and specimen fit.
  • Operational lead (administrator, director of nursing or practice manager): confirms logistics, reporting and billing.

The consultation takes about 15 minutes per panel.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to change laboratories?

No. InnovexLab can be added for a single panel while your current laboratory continues to handle other testing.

What does onboarding cost?

Onboarding is free, and we provide the collection supplies needed to begin. Ordered tests are billed separately, and we review the billing path with you before the first order.

How is testing billed?

For Medicare Part B and insured patients, we bill the payer directly, with no invoice to your facility. Residents in a covered Medicare Part A skilled nursing stay fall under consolidated billing: the facility, not Medicare, pays the lab for those tests. We review that arrangement with your administrator before you order.

Uninsured? Ask us about self-pay options.

How are specimens transported?

Specimen transport is arranged with your team during onboarding.

How are results reported?

Results are reported to the ordering provider. The reporting method is confirmed during setup.

Which specimens does each panel require?

Accepted specimen types and collection steps are reviewed with the ordering clinician during consultation, to prevent rejected specimens.

Who do we contact with questions?

The laboratory directly: (352) 328-3066 or info@innovex-lab.com.
