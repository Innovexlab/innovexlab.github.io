Editorial policy

The organism library, the clinical overview, the resistance-gene guide and the patient page summarize guidance published by public-health agencies (chiefly the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, CDC) and professional societies (chiefly the Infectious Diseases Society of America, IDSA). Each page lists the sources it draws on. Treatment considerations are given at drug-class level only; doses and regimens are left to the current guideline and the treating clinician.

Panel pages list targets as printed on InnovexLab’s requisition forms. A listed target describes what a panel is designed to detect; it is not a statement about any individual patient’s result.

Content was last updated in November 2026. To report an error, call (352) 328-3066 or email info@innovex-lab.com. Please don’t include patient information.

Medical disclaimer

This page is general educational information summarized from published public-health and professional-society guidance. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment recommendation, and reading it does not create a clinician–patient relationship with InnovexLab. Decisions about testing, diagnosis and treatment belong to the treating clinician, using the patient’s history and examination, laboratory and susceptibility results, local resistance patterns and current guidelines. Guidance changes over time; consult the original sources for the latest version. InnovexLab provides this information as is and does not warrant that it is complete or current.

Patients with questions about their health should contact their own healthcare provider. In an emergency, call 911.
